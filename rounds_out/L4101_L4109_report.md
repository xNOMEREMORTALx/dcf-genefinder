**Graded:** T1 FAIL, T2 FAIL, T3 FAIL. Two tones did not beat one (mean |error| 0.017 vs 0.016).

**The important finding.**
- **The error is identical** for a 2 s and a separate 10 s video (different seed, different length).
- **So the ±0.02 is a fixed bias of the method, not noise.** More data and more tones cannot remove it.
- **Practically ±0.02 is fine.** L4065 recovered an unknown tone to 0.15 Hz with it. Calibration work stops here unless a real phone shows a larger error.

## L4105: which Φ_m(10ᵏ)/d are figurate? (1 of 2)
**Setup:** m = 1–12, k = 1–20, small divisors d, seven families.

**Results:**
- **C1 PASS.** Φ₃(10ᵏ)/3 is centred hexagonal for every k (the code agrees with the theorem).
- **C2 FAIL.** I predicted no other large hits. But Φ₉(10ᵏ)/3 is also centred hexagonal for every k. That is no new family: Φ₉(x) = Φ₃(x³), so it is the same theorem at 3k. The same holds for every Φ_{3ʲ}.
- **One genuinely different large hit:** Φ₂(10³) = 1001 is the 26th pentagonal number (26·77/2). It is isolated; no other k gives a pentagonal number. [D]
- **Small hits (≤ 1000, ignored by design):** 91 = Φ₆(10) is both triangular and centred hexagonal; 13 = Φ₆(10)/7 is both a centred square and a star number.

**Conclusion.** Among cyclotomic values of powers of 10, the 37-family is the only infinite figurate family in this census.

## L4109: `dcf_corner.py`, a corner camera from one phone photo (1 of 2)
**Setup:** synthetic wall photos with two hidden lamps, ambient light, vignetting and 1% noise.

**Results:**
- **K1 PASS.** With exact geometry, both lamps were found within 1 patch in **9 of 10**.
- **K2 FAIL.** With the occluder misplaced by 2 cm, only **5 of 10** within 2 patches (needed 6).
- **Lesson:** the occluder edge position must be measured to about 1 cm.

**The tool:** `dcf_corner.py` in dcf-genefinder (and in dcf-math/phone). Usage is in its header and in the experiment kit.

## Summary
- **Row-timing calibration has a fixed bias of about ±0.02** that more tones or longer videos cannot remove. It is small enough for recovering tones, so calibration work is closed.
- **The 37-family (with its Φ_{3ʲ} copies) is the only infinite figurate family** among Φ_m(10ᵏ)/d, m ≤ 12. 1001 is an isolated pentagonal hit.
- **The corner-camera tool works in simulation** when the occluder is measured to about 1 cm.
